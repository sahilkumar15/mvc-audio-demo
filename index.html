Abstract

MambaVoiceCloning (MVC) investigates whether the conditioning pathway of diffusion-based text-to-speech can be made fully state-space-model based at inference, removing attention and explicit recurrent modules across text, rhythm, and prosody while preserving speech quality. MVC combines a gated bidirectional Mamba text encoder, a Temporal Bi-Mamba supervised by a lightweight alignment teacher used only during training, and an Expressive Mamba with AdaLN modulation.

Under a fixed StyleTTS2 mel-diffusion-vocoder backbone, MVC provides linear-time O(T) conditioning with bounded activation memory and supports practical finite look-ahead streaming. The model is trained on LJSpeech and LibriTTS and evaluated on zero-shot speakers, cross-lingual speech, out-of-distribution text, and long-form synthesis.

MVC achieves modest but statistically reliable improvements in speech quality and conditioning efficiency under controlled comparisons. Its deployed encoder contains 21M parameters and achieves a 1.6× improvement in encoder throughput, while the diffusion decoder remains the dominant source of inference latency.
